Privacy Policy

arunsanjeev.dev is a personal portfolio operated by M S Arun Sanjeev. It does not require an account, does not set advertising or tracking cookies, and does not sell or share personal data.

Contact form. If you submit the contact form, your name, email address and message are sent to the Web3Forms service, which forwards them to Arun’s email inbox. They are used only to reply to you and are not added to any mailing list. You can ask for your message to be deleted at any time by emailing msarunsanjeev@gmail.com.

Third-party services. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages behind Cloudflare, which process standard request data such as IP address and user agent for delivery and security. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, icons from unpkg, an embedded Google Map on the contact section, and blog posts from Medium through the rss2json API. Each of these providers has its own privacy policy.

Changes and questions. This policy may be updated as the site changes; the current version is always at this address. Questions about privacy can be sent to msarunsanjeev@gmail.com.
