About M S Arun Sanjeev

M S Arun Sanjeev (also written Arun Sanjeev) is a Software Developer and Full Stack Developer based in Namakkal, Tamil Nadu, India. He is a final-year Computer Science and Engineering student at M. Kumarasamy College of Engineering, Karur (2023–2027), and builds web and mobile applications, cloud deployments, cyber security tooling and Generative AI products.

Alongside his studies he is the founder of Aalvinai Technologies, co-founder and software developer at Triospark IT Solutions, an Associate Student Ambassador at Microsoft, a Core Team Member of the AWS Student Builder Group, and Vice Chair of the IEEE Systems, Man, and Cybernetics Society. He has won several national hackathons, holds 17 certifications, and co-authored the IEEE ICCTAC 2026 paper “DIGSAFE: 5G-Based Smart Helmet for Proactive Worker Safety using XAI”.

This website is his personal portfolio. It lists his projects, experience, education, certifications, achievements, publications and blog posts. Everything on it is written and maintained by him, and the same information is available on his public profiles on GitHub, LinkedIn and Medium. For work enquiries, see the contact page.
