# Hackathons: M S Arun Sanjeev

M S Arun Sanjeev (Arun Sanjeev) is a Software Developer from Namakkal, Tamil Nadu, and a final-year Computer Science and Engineering student at M. Kumarasamy College of Engineering, Karur. Between 2025 and 2026 he won six hackathon and appathon titles, most of them with Team Cyber Assaulterz: the Hexaware AI Innovation League (HAIL 2026) as a solo entrant among 500+ participants, AI Ascend 2026 powered by Kyndryl and AWS (1st prize, ₹30,000), Prathiyogitha Vol 2 at Kongu Engineering College (1st prize, ₹10,000), the Thiran 2026 Appathon at Sri Eshwar College of Engineering, and both Hackspire and Appthon at Gencraft 2025. He was also runner-up at the 36-hour Hackfest 2026 and competed in national hackathons run by Google Developer Groups (Build for Bangalore, 30 hours) and Major League Hacking (HackPrix Season 3, 36 hours).
