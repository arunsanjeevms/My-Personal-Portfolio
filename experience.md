# Experience: M S Arun Sanjeev

M S Arun Sanjeev (Arun Sanjeev) is the founder of Aalvinai Technologies, a Tamil Nadu software company building AI, ERP, CRM and FinTech systems, which he started in September 2026. Before that he co-founded Triospark IT Solutions, where he shipped production SaaS products such as ZionHR and managed VPS, CI/CD and Cloudflare infrastructure. Alongside his Computer Science and Engineering degree at M. Kumarasamy College of Engineering, Karur, he is an Associate Student Ambassador at Microsoft (after the Alpha and Beta Microsoft Learn Student Ambassador milestones), a Core Team Member of the AWS Student Builder Group, and Vice Chair of the IEEE Systems, Man, and Cybernetics Society. Earlier roles include campus ambassador programmes with Unstop, GeeksforGeeks, MyGov India, E-Cell IIT Bombay and Techfest IIT Bombay.
